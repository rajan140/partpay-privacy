AT A GLANCE

PartPay helps you plan and track. Your UPI app makes the payment.

PartPay splits a total amount into smaller parts of up to ₹1,999 and guides you through paying them, one part at a time, using a UPI app installed on your phone.

PartPay does not send money, know who you pay, check payments, read your UPI PIN or access your bank details. The status of each part in PartPay is what you mark after returning from your UPI app.

1What is saved on your phone

PartPay saves only what it needs to keep your split plan and settings between app sessions.

Split progress

The total amount, the payment parts, the status of each part, and a UTR if you choose to enter one.

App settings

Whether you finished the intro, your theme, the “Remember selected UPI app” setting and the UPI app you used last.

This is saved in PartPay’s private storage on your phone. PartPay does not send it to SS Pixels or to any server.

2What PartPay never touches

Bank and payment details

No UPI PIN, bank login, card details, OTPs or bank account details.

Personal phone data

No SMS, contacts, camera, photos, files, location, microphone or call logs. PartPay asks for none of these permissions.

The person you pay

You choose the recipient inside your UPI app. PartPay does not ask for or save this.

Payment results

PartPay receives no payment result from your bank or UPI app, so it cannot confirm whether a payment went through.

3UPI apps and clipboard

Installed UPI apps

PartPay looks for the UPI apps installed on your phone so it can show them as choices. It then opens the app you pick. PartPay does not start or process a payment for you.

Clipboard

When you pick a UPI app or tap “Just copy the amount”, PartPay copies the amount of the current part to your phone’s clipboard, so you can paste it in your UPI app. Android may show a short “copied” message. Only the amount is copied.

4Sharing and internet use

  • No account: there is no sign-up or login.
  • No ads or analytics: PartPay uses no advertising or analytics tools.
  • Nothing sent to SS Pixels: your split plan and payment records stay on your phone.
  • No selling of data: SS Pixels does not sell or share PartPay user data.

When you leave PartPay to pay in a UPI app, that app and its payment partners follow their own terms and privacy policies. Please read their policies to understand how they handle your data.

5How long data is kept

An unfinished split and your settings stay on your phone, so you can continue later. A finished plan is cleared when you tap Done. An active plan is cleared when you tap Discard.

You can remove all PartPay data at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may restore your settings when you reinstall PartPay.

6Your choices

Because your PartPay data is saved only on your phone, you are always in control of it:

  • See it: your split plan and its parts are shown in the app.
  • Change it: edit a split before you start paying, or change your settings at any time.
  • Delete it: tap Discard or Done, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app.

SS Pixels keeps no PartPay data on its own servers. If you have a privacy question or request, including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, email us and we will reply.

7How your data is protected

PartPay keeps its data in its private storage on your phone and handles as little data as possible. It never collects bank details or UPI PINs.

Tip: before paying a part again, always check the payment history in your UPI app or bank app first. That history is the final record of every payment.

8Children

PartPay is made for adults who make UPI payments. It has no accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
