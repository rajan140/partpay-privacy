AT A GLANCE

PartPay is a planner and tracker — not a payment processor

PartPay splits a total amount into smaller parts and guides you through paying them using a UPI app installed on your device.

PartPay does not send money, know your recipient, verify payments, read your UPI PIN, or access your bank credentials. Payment status in PartPay is based on what you report after returning from your UPI app.

1. Information stored on your device

PartPay stores only the information needed to keep your split plan and preferences working between app sessions.

Split progress

Total amount, payment parts, their statuses, and an optional UTR that you choose to enter.

App preferences

Onboarding status, theme preference, “Remember selected UPI app” setting, and the last-used UPI app.

This information is stored locally in Android app storage. PartPay does not transmit this stored information to SS Pixels or to a PartPay server.

2. Information PartPay does not access

Financial credentials

No UPI PIN, bank login credentials, card details, OTPs, or bank account credentials.

Personal device data

No SMS, contacts, camera, photos/files, location, microphone, or call logs.

Recipient information

PartPay does not ask for or store the person you choose to pay inside your UPI app.

Payment verification

PartPay does not receive a payment result from your bank or UPI app and cannot independently verify payment success.

3. Installed UPI apps and clipboard

Installed UPI apps

PartPay checks which compatible payment apps are installed so it can show them as options. This is app discovery only. PartPay opens the app you choose; it does not initiate or process a payment on your behalf.

Clipboard

When you choose a payment app or use “Just copy the amount,” PartPay copies the current payment-part amount to your device clipboard so you can paste it into your UPI app.

4. Data collection, sharing and network use

  • No PartPay account: no account creation or sign-in.
  • No analytics or advertising: PartPay does not use analytics or advertising tracking.
  • No PartPay network transmission: the app does not send your split plan or payment-tracking data to SS Pixels.
  • No sale of personal data: SS Pixels does not sell PartPay user data.

When you leave PartPay and use a third-party UPI app, that app and its payment providers operate under their own terms and privacy practices. PartPay does not control their data handling.

5. Data retention and deletion

An unfinished split and your app preferences can remain on your device so you can continue later. A completed plan is cleared when you select Done, and an active plan is cleared when you select Discard.

You can also remove locally stored PartPay data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app, subject to your device's Android backup/restore settings.

6. Security

PartPay minimizes data handling by keeping its working data locally on your device and by not collecting bank credentials or UPI PINs. Always verify payment status in your UPI or banking app before retrying a payment.

7. Children's privacy

PartPay does not provide user accounts and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a PartPay server.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if PartPay's features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when the policy changes.
